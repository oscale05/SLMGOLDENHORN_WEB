# i18n Strategy

- Languages: ES (default, `/es/`), EN (`/en/`).
- Angular i18n with `@angular/localize` or Transloco; lazy-loaded translation files.
- URL prefix per language; canonical + hreflang alternate links.
- Backend messages localized by `lang` param.
- Legal documents translated to EN (phase 2; ES authoritative).
- Date/number formatting per locale.
